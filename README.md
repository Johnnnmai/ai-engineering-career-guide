## 🔄 Career Transitions

5 paths into AI engineering, each with timeline, what transfers, and what to learn.

| From | Timeline | Your Superpower |
|------|----------|----------------|
| [Software Engineer](learning-paths/from-software-engineer.md) | 2-3 months | Production systems, deployment, debugging |
| [Data Scientist](learning-paths/from-data-scientist.md) | 2-3 months | ML theory, statistics, model training |
| [ML Engineer](learning-paths/from-ml-engineer.md) | 1-2 months | Model deployment, MLOps, production ML |
| [ML Researcher](learning-paths/from-researcher.md) | 3-5 months | Deep theory, novel architectures, paper reading |
